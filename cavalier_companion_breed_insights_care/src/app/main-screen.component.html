Cavalier Companion: Breed Insights & Care

Breed Overview

The Cavalier King Charles Spaniel is a graceful, affectionate, and friendly companion, renowned for its expressive eyes and charming demeanor. Originating in the United Kingdom, this toy breed is known for being adaptable, sociable, and excellent with children and other pets. Cavaliers thrive on companionship and are happiest when close to their families.

  • Origin: United Kingdom
  • Temperament: Gentle, affectionate, sociable
  • Size: 12–13 inches, 13–18 lbs
  • Lifespan: 12–15 years

Care Tips

  • Grooming: Brush their silky coat several times a week. Check ears regularly for cleanliness.
  • Exercise: Provide daily walks and gentle play. Cavaliers enjoy moderate activity but are not overly energetic.
  • Feeding: Offer high-quality dog food in measured amounts to avoid overeating.
  • Health: Schedule regular vet visits. Watch for heart, ear, and eye issues common to the breed.
  • Affection & Training: Use positive reinforcement. These dogs respond best to gentle guidance and thrive on attention.
